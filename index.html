About

How I work

I am a developer from Manisa, Türkiye, working mostly on mobile. Most of what I ship is small on purpose: a utility that does one thing, or a package that removes one recurring problem from an app I was already building.

When a piece of an app turns out to be worth reusing, it becomes its own package with its own tests, CI and documentation, rather than staying buried in a repository. That is where ads_kit and cupertino_liquid_glass came from.

Everything I release is open source, and the things that reach users are signed, notarized and shipped through the channels their platform expects.
